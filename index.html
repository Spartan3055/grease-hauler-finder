How to use these pages

  1. Open your city or utility list and check the official revision date.
  2. Verify on the official PDF or program page that a company is still listed.
  3. Ask the hauler for a city- or utility-approved manifest after the pump-out.
  4. Record the work in our log, or in the form your city provides, and set the next reminder.

Haulers who want to be notified when paid leads open can read the For haulers placeholder page. Nothing is being collected there yet.
