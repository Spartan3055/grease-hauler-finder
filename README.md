# FOG File

Free, unofficial pages that republish official city and utility **certified / registered / permitted grease-hauler lists**, plus a browser-based pump-out reminder and manifest log.

**Live site:** https://spartan3055.github.io/grease-hauler-finder/

FOG File is not affiliated with the City of Tampa, Louisville MSD, HRSD, or any hauler named on the site. Official lists change — always verify with the issuing city or utility before hiring. This is not legal advice.

Lists currently covered:

- [Tampa, FL](https://spartan3055.github.io/grease-hauler-finder/tampa/) — City of Tampa registered list (revised 09/02/25), 41 companies
- [Louisville, KY](https://spartan3055.github.io/grease-hauler-finder/louisville/) — MSD certified list (export Sept. 2026), 34 companies
- [Hampton Roads, VA](https://spartan3055.github.io/grease-hauler-finder/hampton-roads/) — HRSD permitted list (October 1, 2026), 27 companies, with city pages for Virginia Beach, Norfolk, Chesapeake, and Newport News

Parsed data is in [`data/haulers.json`](data/haulers.json). Dated official PDF copies are in [`data/archive/`](data/archive/).
