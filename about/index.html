About FOG File

A free site that republishes official grease-hauler lists in a form kitchens can actually use, and a private pump-out log you can bookmark.

FOG stands for fats, oils, and grease. Most sewer utilities require commercial kitchens to keep grease-control equipment pumped out and to hire haulers that the city or utility has certified, registered, or permitted. Those official lists are often PDFs. FOG File turns the current official file into a searchable page, shows the revision date, and links back to the source.

We are not a government agency, not a hauler, and not affiliated with the City of Tampa, Louisville MSD, or HRSD. We do not certify or approve anyone.

How lists are updated

We download the official PDF the program page points to, keep a dated archive copy with a SHA-256 checksum in this site’s repository, and parse the public business information printed on the list (name, phone, and, where the list includes them, fax, email, address, and service types). We do not add companies from ads, directories, or word of mouth.

Lists last retrieved: November 18, 2026. If a list on this site is older than the file now posted by the city or utility, treat the official file as correct.

Sources

  • Louisville MSD, Certified Grease Waste Hauler List (Export: Sept. 2026): official PDF, FOG program page.
  • HRSD, Permitted Grease Haulers (October 01, 2026): official PDF, linked from the Non-Residential Customer FAQs.
